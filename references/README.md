# References

Texts and papers used for conventions, formulas and independent checks. The code does not depend on any of them; formulas taken from the literature are labelled as such in the module docstrings and are validated by tests against the package's own calculations.

* C. W. Misner, K. S. Thorne, J. A. Wheeler, *Gravitation* (1973): conventions, geodesics, tidal tensor, Schwarzschild orbits.
* R. M. Wald, *General Relativity* (1984): variational principle, boundary terms, linearised gravity, Killing vectors.
* S. M. Carroll, *Spacetime and Geometry* (2004): index conventions, Bianchi identities, linearised waves.
* S. Weinberg, *Gravitation and Cosmology* (1972): Newtonian limit, light deflection, Shapiro delay.
* J. M. Bardeen, W. H. Press, S. A. Teukolsky, *Astrophys. J.* 178, 347 (1972): Kerr circular orbits and ISCO (formulas used as inputs).
* V. Bozza, *Phys. Rev. D* 66, 103001 (2002): strong-deflection limit (used in a test).
* C. P. Burgess, *Living Rev. Relativ.* 7, 5 (2004): quantum gravity as an effective field theory.
* J. Polchinski, *String Theory* vols. 1 and 2 (1998): the sigma-model beta functions (explanatory page only).
* B. Palais, *Commun. Math. Phys.* 69, 19 (1979): principle of symmetric criticality.
* R. A. Isaacson, *Phys. Rev.* 166, 1263 (1968): effective stress-energy of gravitational waves (context for the second-order result).

Please verify page and equation numbers against your own copies; they are not reproduced here.
